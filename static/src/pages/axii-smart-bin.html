03 Process

How it works, tap to alert

Eight steps run automatically every time someone uses the bin.

1

Tap RFID card

User taps their registered RFID card at the reader on the lid.

2

Verify user

The RC522 module checks the card against authorized users.

3

Lid opens

On a valid tap, the MG995 servo motor lifts the lid automatically.

4

Deposit waste

The user drops their waste into the bin.

5

Measure weight

The load cell and HX711 amplifier record the added weight.

6

Check fill level

The HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor measures remaining bin capacity.

7

Send data to AXII Central App

The ESP32 transmits user, weight, and fill data over Wi-Fi.

8

Dashboard & alerts

AXII Central App logs the record and triggers alerts where needed.
